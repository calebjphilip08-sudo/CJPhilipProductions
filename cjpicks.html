CJ Picks

Here are a few of the tools, influences, and creative habits that shape the work I make at CJ Philip Productions.

Creative Tools

Tools that help ideas become work

  • Reedsy: I use Reedsy for drafting my writing projects, essays, and long-form creative work. Reedsy.com
  • Google Docs: I use Google Docs for planning, outlining ideas, and organising projects before they become fully developed. Google Docs
  • Visual Studio Code: I use VS Code to build and refine this website and other creative digital projects. Visual Studio Code
  • Ibis Paint: I use Ibis Paint for digital artwork, concept sketches, and visual experimentation. Ibis Paint

Recommended Reading.

Books that I love.

  • Percy Jackson and the Olympians series by Rick Riordan. An engaging blend of mythology and modern reality.
  • The Lord of the Rings & The Hobbit by J.R.R Tolkein. The Foundation of modern fantasy literature.
  • Mistborn trilogy by Brandon Sanderson. A masterful example of worldbuilding and magic systems.
