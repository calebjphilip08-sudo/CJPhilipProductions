Alvora

Project Overview

Alvora is a fictional world from CJ Philip Productions. More details will be shared here as the project grows.

What is the world of Alvora?

Alvora asks a simply question. What if a fantasy world didn't become stagnant after reaching medevial times? And what is the magic didn't die?
