Profile

Hello, I am CJ Philip.

I was born and raised in Scotland, and I live with ADHD, dyslexia, and dyspraxia. Over the years, I have faced many challenges, but creativity has always been one of the clearest and most meaningful parts of my life.

Through CJ Philip Productions, I am sharing my stories, ideas, and creative vision with the world. I believe in building work that is personal, honest, and rooted in human imagination.

Creative studio

What CJ Philip Productions Does

At CJ Philip Productions, I focus on creating work that is authentic, human-made, and intentionally crafted. None of the work produced under this name is created using generative AI.

I believe in the value of craftsmanship, creativity, and originality. CJ Philip Productions is where my ideas, writing, artwork, and creative ambitions take shape and come to life.

Identity

What Is CJ Philip Productions?

CJ Philip Productions is not a company in the traditional sense; it is the creative identity I use for my projects, ideas, and artistic work. While my name is closely associated with the brand, it represents a larger creative process that is deeply personal to me.

This creative space is an important part of how I develop stories, explore ideas, and build the worlds and projects that matter to me most.
